Two lines, 288 characters: a traffic jam explained to a 10-year-old through marbles and a drinking straw — then an honest second line about what the analogy maps and what it leaves out.

> A traffic jam is like trying to pour a whole bag of marbles through a drinking straw — every car wants through the same tiny opening at once.
>
> The straw shows why one slow car backs everyone up, but it leaves out that drivers, unlike marbles, can choose to leave gaps or take another road.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py --self-test
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), validates it against the gate (1–400 characters, exactly two lines, one sentence per line, not only white space), and reads it back to confirm the file holds exactly the entry. Standard library only, deterministic, no network, no clock.
